MITRE ATT&CK Mappings
● MAPPED WHERE SUPPORTED BY EVIDENCE

CISA KEV Alignment
● CISA ALIGNED
CISA KEV records can inform risk prioritization when the source catalog is available and current. Verify the KEV source timestamp and matching vulnerability identity before relying on a priority decision.

  • KEV flags on verified matching advisories
  • EPSS + KEV combined risk scoring model
  • Escalation according to the validated vulnerability-priority policy
